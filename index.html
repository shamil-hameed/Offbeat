Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Information Gathering Baseline

Offfbeat operates as a free open-source itinerary indexing resource. We explicitly do not track personal identifying metrics or sell biometric vectors to external programmatic ad layers.

2. Custom Canvas Persistence Data

Any custom itinerary built via the Compare & Customize screen relies entirely on local memory allocations inside your own client browser wrapper. No travel parameters are parsed onto third-party analytical pipelines.

3. Public Map Integrations

Geospatial route visualizations utilize Leaflet alongside OpenStreetMap CDN scripts. These modules serve basic lightweight geographic display functions and do not harvest background persistent user coordinates data layers.

4. Contact & Governance Queries

For data validation requests regarding community-contributed routes or administrative concerns, connect immediately to our engineering team mailbox at offbeattravels33@gmail.com.
