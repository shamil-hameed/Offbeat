Contribute a Place

WHY CONTRIBUTE TO OFFBEAT?

Popular travel sites send everyone to the same crowded spots. By sharing your offbeat discoveries, hidden viewpoints, and budget-friendly routes, you help fellow travelers experience authentic places while keeping local travel knowledge free for everyone.

How to Submit Your Route:

1. Prepare Your Details: Gather the place name, a short description, your day-by-day itinerary notes, a Google Maps link, and 3–4 clear photos.

2. Send via Email: Email your details to our curation team using the address below.

3. Verification & Publishing: Once we verify the route details, we will publish your itinerary on Offbeat with full credit to your name.

Submission Email Address: offbeattravels33@gmail.com
